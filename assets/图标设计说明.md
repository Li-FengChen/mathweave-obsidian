# 数学知识织网 · 图标设计

制作方式：内置 imagegen 图像生成工具；透明背景。程序图标使用 app-icon.png，Windows 图标使用由其转换的 app.ico，包含 16 至 256 像素的多个尺寸。

设计：深靛蓝圆角方形，暖白积分符号，青色与淡紫色的知识节点和连线。积分表达数学，节点表达知识关联；图形减少细节，适合桌面和任务栏的小尺寸显示。

实际生成提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final Windows desktop app icon, square 1024x1024 PNG with true transparency outside the icon silhouette. Primary request: a simple beautiful thoughtfully designed icon for a Chinese mathematics conversation-to-knowledge-notes desktop app. One rounded-square dark midnight-indigo tile, a clean warm-ivory mathematical integral glyph in the center whose curve subtly resembles a flowing notebook ribbon; three small cyan/lavender knowledge nodes joined by thin clean arcs alongside the integral, suggesting linked mathematical knowledge. Flat vector-like geometric design, sharp readable edges, generous negative space, restrained premium editorial feel, high contrast recognizable at 32px. No text, no letters other than the integral mathematical mark, no mockup, no perspective, no decorative background, no watermark, no excessive gradients or tiny details. Centered single icon occupying around 86% of canvas with rounded square corners, keep outside transparent.

输出已经应用到主窗口、任务栏、Windows 可执行文件和项目启动快捷方式。
